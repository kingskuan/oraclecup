# OracleCup — Brand Brief

## What it is
The first AI-Agent–powered prediction market for the FIFA World Cup, built natively on X Layer.

## Tagline (pick one)
1. **"The AI Oracle of the World Cup. On-chain."** ← primary
2. "Predict the Cup. Trust the Oracle."
3. "Where AI calls the match. And you cash the bet."

## One-liner (for Twitter bio, judges, pitch)
> OracleCup is an AI Agent that posts live World Cup odds, runs prediction markets on X Layer, and auto-settles winners on-chain — all in one tweet-to-trade flow.

## Why it wins
- **Innovation**: First product to fuse autonomous AI Agent + on-chain prediction market on X Layer
- **Market**: World Cup is the largest sports event on Earth — billions of attention units → on-chain bets
- **Completion**: Smart contract deployed, dApp live, AI Agent posting, demo video
- **Onchain-native**: Every market, bet, and settlement is verifiable on X Layer
